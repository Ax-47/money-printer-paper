This project presents an application of the end-to-end data science process to analyze key factors influencing English Premier League match outcomes and to build machine learning models for predicting match results in three outcome classes: Home Win, Draw, and Away Win.

The methodology integrates multi-source historical match data spanning from 2014 to 2026, encompassing overall match statistics, starting lineups, player availability and injury histories, and Expected Goals (xG) metrics. The aggregated dataset underwent rigorous data cleaning, relational merging, and feature engineering to construct essential predictive features, including Elo ratings, xG metrics, recent team form, betting market odds, and key player unavailability indicators. Classification algorithms, specifically Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, and Random Forest, were subsequently trained and evaluated.

The experimental results indicate that incorporating player-level availability alongside advanced statistical metrics (xG) and team strength indicators significantly enhances predictive accuracy compared to traditional models relying solely on basic historical match outcomes. Among the evaluated models, Random Forest achieved the highest predictive performance, demonstrating high alignment with betting market trends while effectively quantifying feature importance rankings. The analytical framework developed in this project provides a robust foundation for processing complex sports data and supporting data-driven decision-making in sports analytics.
